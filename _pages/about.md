I am a tenure-track Assistant Professor in the [Department of Industrial Engineering](https://www.ie.tsinghua.edu.cn/eng/) at Tsinghua University. Previously, I was a postdoctoral researcher at the University of Pennsylvania, working with Professor [Yuxin Chen](https://yuxinchen2020.github.io). I received my Ph.D. in [Industrial and Operations Engineering](https://ioe.engin.umich.edu) from the University of Michigan, where I was fortunate to be advised by Professor [Salar Fattahi](https://fattahi.engin.umich.edu).

## Research interests

AI for optimization and optimization for AI.

## Join my group

I am looking for prospective Ph.D. students and can recruit **one Ph.D. student per year**. Research assistants are also welcome. If you are interested, please contact me by email.
